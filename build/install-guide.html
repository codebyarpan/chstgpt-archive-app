ChatGPT Archive

Keeps a copy of your ChatGPT conversations on your Mac, so you can search them, sort them into groups and read them any time. Everything stays on your Mac.

You need: a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or newer), Google Chrome, and an internet connection the first time you open the app.
Not sure which chip you have? Click the Apple menu → About This Mac. It should say Chip: Apple M…

1 Install the app

  1. Double-click the file ChatGPT-Archive-….dmg you received. A window opens.
  2. Drag the ChatGPT Archive icon onto the Applications folder in that window.
  3. Close the window. In Finder's sidebar, click the eject button ⏏ next to "ChatGPT Archive".

2 Open it the first time (one-time step)

The app doesn't come from the App Store, so your Mac asks you to confirm once that you trust it.

  1. Open your Applications folder and double-click ChatGPT Archive.
  2. A message says Apple could not verify the app. Click Done (do not click "Move to Bin").
  3. Open System Settings (Apple menu → System Settings) → Privacy & Security.
  4. Scroll down. Next to "ChatGPT Archive" was blocked…, click Open Anyway. Enter your Mac password if asked.
  5. Click Open Anyway once more. The app opens. You never have to do this again.
On older Macs (macOS 14 or earlier) you can instead right-click (or Control-click) the app → Open → Open.

3 Wait for the first setup

The first time, the app downloads a few components (about 150 MB). This usually takes one to five minutes. Keep the internet on. If it shows "Something needs attention", check your Wi-Fi and click Try again.

4 Connect Google Chrome

The app shows four simple steps on its Home screen. Follow them in order:

  1. Open in Chrome: opens Chrome's extensions page.
  2. Turn on the Developer mode switch (top-right of that page).
  3. Show the extension folder, then drag the folder ChatGPT Archive Extension onto the Chrome page.
  4. Open chatgpt.com (reload it if it was open). A round green button appears at the bottom-right.
Please don't move or delete the folder ChatGPT Archive Extension in your home folder: Chrome uses it.

5 Save your chats

  1. On chatgpt.com, click the round green button at the bottom-right.
  2. Tick the chats you want (or click not saved yet to tick all new ones). You can type in the box to find a chat.
  3. Optionally pick a group (like a folder), then press Save.

Viewing a chat? The panel shows This chat · Save it at the top: one click saves it.

✓ Everyday use

  • Search: type words or a question ("what did we decide about the logo?"). It also finds chats that mean the same thing in other words. Shortcut: ⌘K.
  • All chats: browse everything; filter by title, ChatGPT project or group. Click a chat to read it.
  • While reading a chat: Add to group, Copy the whole chat, Open in ChatGPT, or delete it from the archive.
  • Groups: create, rename or delete groups. Deleting a group never deletes chats.
The app keeps running in the menu bar (the small box icon at the top-right of your screen) after you close its window, so Chrome can always save chats. It also starts by itself when you log in (you can turn that off in Settings & help). To quit completely: click the menu bar icon → Quit ChatGPT Archive.

If something isn't working

No green button on chatgpt.comReload the page. Check that step 4 was done (Settings & help in the app shows the steps again).
"The ChatGPT Archive app is not running"Open ChatGPT Archive from Applications, then click Try again in the panel.
Chrome shows a "developer mode extensions" messageClose it with the ✕. Don't click "Disable".
A chat changed in ChatGPTSave it again; the newer version replaces the old one and keeps its groups.
Anything elseIn the app: Settings & help → Advanced → Show log files, and send those files to the person who gave you the app.

Optional: if you use the Claude app, Settings & help → Connect Claude lets Claude search your saved ChatGPT chats when you ask it to.
